How It Works

Three simple steps to make your road safer

1
Spot the Hazard

Notice a pothole, broken sign, waterlogging, or any road hazard? Take a photo and note the location.

2
Submit a Report

Fill in the details, drop a pin on the map, and submit your report. It takes less than 2 minutes.

3
Track Progress

Get your unique Report ID and track it in real time as authorities review and resolve the hazard.
